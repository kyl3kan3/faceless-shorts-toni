### Idea engine (when the list runs low)
For any concept a 4-year-old asks "why/what/how" about, ask: *can Willow show it with
one picture and 6 short lines?* If yes, it's an episode. Keep one idea per video.
Seasonal hooks (counting pumpkins in October, hearts in February) ride trends safely.
