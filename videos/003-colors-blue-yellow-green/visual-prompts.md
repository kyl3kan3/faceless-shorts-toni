# Visual prompts — Color Magic: Blue + Yellow = Green

Append config `style.image_suffix` to every beat. 9:16, one clear subject, **no text in
image**. **Pass Willow's model sheet as a reference on every beat.** Higgsfield
`nano_banana_2`, aspect "9:16".

- **B1 (hook):** Willow with a painter's beret and a mischievous happy grin, holding a
  paintbrush, blank pastel canvas behind — _motion: gentle lean-in, brush wiggle_
- **B2:** Willow beside one big glossy BLUE paint blob — _motion: blue blob jiggles_
- **B3:** Willow beside a big glossy YELLOW paint blob (blue still in frame, left) —
  _motion: yellow blob jiggles in_
- **B4:** the blue and yellow blobs sliding toward each other, Willow watching wide-eyed
  — _motion: blobs slide together, anticipation_
- **B5:** the blobs swirl and become one big GREEN blob, Willow cheering with sparkle
  tail, little stars — _motion: swirl + sparkle reveal_
- **B6 (payoff):** Willow waving beside the green blob next to a tuft of green grass —
  _motion: slow pull-out, wave_
