# Script — Color Magic: Blue + Yellow = Green 🎨

**Series:** Color Magic · **Promise:** mixing two colors makes a new one ·
**Payoff:** "Blue and yellow make green!" · **Target:** ~34s · ~70 words · 6 beats ·
**Made for Kids: YES**

[B1 — HOOK 0–3s]
**[Willow]** "Want to see some color magic? Watch this!"

[B2 · blue paint blob]
**[Mama]** "Here is BLUE. Blue like the sky."

[B3 · yellow paint blob]
**[Mama]** "And here is YELLOW. Yellow like the sun."

[B4 · the two blobs slide together]
**[Leo]** "What happens if we mix them?" **[Willow]** "Let's find out!"

[B5 · they swirl into GREEN]
**[Mama + Willow]** "Ta-da! Blue and yellow make... GREEN!"

[B6 — PAYOFF + CTA]
**[Willow]** "Green like the grass! You made color magic! Come learn with Willow!"

---
## Plain narration (→ audio/narration.txt; single-voice fallback)
Want to see some color magic? Watch this. Here is blue. Blue like the sky. And here is
yellow. Yellow like the sun. What happens if we mix them? Let's find out. Ta-da! Blue
and yellow make green! Green like the grass. You made color magic! Come learn with Willow!

## Audio build
Multi-voice: render per voice, `edit_audio_stitch`. Put a tiny "magic sparkle" SFX on
B5. Single-voice fallback via narration.txt. Music: brighten the bed on the B5 reveal.

## On-screen overlay
Color word labels (BLUE, YELLOW, GREEN) in their own colors as each appears; a "+" and
"=" between the blobs to teach the mixing as a tiny equation.
