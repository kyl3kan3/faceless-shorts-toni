# SEO & Discovery — getting Willow found

How videos get discovered drives everything downstream (reach → audience → money).
This is the packaging playbook. (Live keyword volumes via Ahrefs/Semrush are gated on
the current plan — when upgraded, validate the seed terms below and paste real numbers
here. The patterns themselves are stable.)

## How kids content actually gets discovered
1. **Search (parents type for the child):** "abc song", "counting for kids",
   "learning colors for toddlers", "nursery rhymes", "phonics for kids". Parents
   search broad, evergreen, branded-by-format terms. **Title for these literally.**
2. **Suggested / "Up next" (autoplay):** the biggest traffic source in kids — a child
   watches one, the platform autoplays similar. You win this with **consistency**
   (same character, same series, similar thumbnails) so the algorithm clusters you,
   and with **strong retention/loops**.
3. **Shorts feed / Reels / FYP:** pure hook + retention. First **2 seconds** decide it.
4. **Home (returning viewers):** a *beloved character* → high return rate → the
   platform shows your new uploads to people who finished your last one.

## Title formulas (use these literally — clarity beats cleverness for kids)
- `Count to 5 with Willow! 🍎 | Counting for Toddlers`
- `The Letter S Sound | Phonics for Kids with Willow 🐍`
- `Blue + Yellow = Green! 🎨 Color Mixing for Kids`
- `Learn Colors with Willow | Toddler Learning Video`
- `Willow's ABC Song 🎵 | Alphabet for Kids`
Rules: put the **learning keyword first** ("Count to 5", "Letter S", "Learn Colors"),
add the **format keyword** parents search ("for toddlers / for kids / phonics /
counting"), keep the **character name** for brand return-rate, one emoji max for shelf
appeal. ≤ ~60 chars visible.

## Seed keywords to target (validate volumes when plan allows)
**Evergreen heads:** abc song · alphabet song · phonics for kids · counting for kids ·
numbers for kids · learning colors / colors for toddlers · shapes for kids · nursery
rhymes · animal sounds · days of the week song · feelings for kids · toddler learning
video · preschool learning.
**Long-tail (easier to rank, high intent):** "count to 10 for toddlers", "letter b
sound words", "mixing colors red and blue", "circle song for kids", "calm down
breathing for kids". Map one episode per long-tail (see `../topics/backlog.md`).

## Thumbnails (the click decision — even autoplay shows them)
- **Willow's face big**, one bright emotion, looking at the viewer.
- **One huge subject/number/letter** (the lesson), brand-teal accent, thick outline.
- **≤ 3 words** of text, fat rounded font, high contrast (readable at phone size).
- **Same template every time** so the channel is instantly recognizable in Suggested.
- Build in Adobe Express/Canva from the episode's hero still.

## Descriptions & tags
- **Description:** 2–3 plain sentences restating what the child learns + the series
  name + a link to the playlist + the website/printable freebie (funnel!).
- **Hashtags:** `#kidslearning #toddler #abcsong #phonics #learnwithwillow` (3–5).
- **Tags:** the seed keywords above + variants ("learn colors", "color song",
  "toddler video", "preschool").
- **Playlists are SEO:** group by series ("Counting with Willow") — playlists rank and
  drive session time (autoplay through the playlist = more watch time).

## Shorts-specific
- **Hook in 2s:** open on Willow's face + the question ("Can you count with me?"),
  never a slow intro. The title sting comes *after* the hook, not before.
- **Loop the ending** back to the first frame (seamless replays inflate views).
- **One idea only.** Re-watchability > information density for this age.
- **Caption the spoken words** (many watch muted; pre-readers + parents read along).

## Consistency = the algorithm's clustering signal
Same character, same series names, same thumbnail template, same upload cadence,
same outro. This is *why* Numberblocks/Cocomelon dominate Suggested — the system
learns "people who like this cluster" and feeds the whole catalog. Be boringly
consistent; it compounds.

## Measure, then double down
Watch **avg % viewed / retention** (Shorts) and **returning-viewer %** + which
**series** and **thumbnail** style win. Use Higgsfield `virality_predictor` on cuts
before posting, and make more of whatever format pops. Kill what doesn't in 3–4 tries.
