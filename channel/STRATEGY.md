# Strategy — the road to $60k/month

## The goal, stated plainly
$60,000/month = **$720k/year**. We do **not** get there from one channel's ad
revenue. We get there by building a small **character IP** — Willow the Wonder Pup —
and monetizing her across platforms and products. Everything else serves that.

## The niche (and why it's good)
**Pre-K / early-elementary foundational learning (ages 2–7)** — counting, ABCs,
phonics, shapes, colors, animals, feelings — delivered as short, character-led
lessons. It matches the family's own kids, it is **evergreen** (a counting video is
as useful in 2030 as today → views compound for years), and it's one of the largest,
most ad-safe corners of the platform.

## Why a recurring character is the whole game
The biggest kids brands are **characters**, not topics:
- **Numberblocks** — math taught by characters that *are* numbers → toys, Netflix, live shows.
- **Cocomelon** — a family + nursery rhymes → one of Earth's most-watched channels → merch/apps/Netflix.
- **Kids Learning Tube / Jools TV / Art with Mati & Dada** — original animated casts that teach, then license out.

A character is an **asset we own**. Topics are commodities. **Willow is our asset** —
and our edge is that she's real (a beloved family dog) voiced by a real family
(the "Ms Rachel" trust signal), produced at AI speed.

## Who we model (and what we take)
| Channel | What we copy |
|---------|--------------|
| Numberblocks | one idea per video; characters *embody* the lesson; tight visual system |
| Cocomelon | song-driven repetition; warm family world; relentless consistency |
| Ms Rachel | slow speech, warmth, parent trust — we get this free from real family voices |
| Kids Learning Tube | sing-along + animation + micro-facts, then music/merch |
| Blippi / fast Shorts | hook in <2s, one idea, loop-friendly ending |

## Positioning decision: parent-trusted, not the COPPA bargain bin
Kids videos are legally "Made for Kids" → low ad money, no comments/memberships
(see [`MONETIZATION.md`](MONETIZATION.md)). So we deliberately also build a **parent
audience and parent products** on properties that are *not* MFK-restricted (a website,
an email list, printables, a membership "The Willow Club"). The kid-facing videos are
the **magnet**; the parent funnel is the **money**. We never cheat the MFK flag — that
carries FTC fines — we just build the business so the flag doesn't cap us.

## Funnel: Shorts are reach, not revenue
1. **Shorts (daily, every platform)** → make Willow famous fast and cheap.
2. → **Long-form** (8–15 min compilations of the same clips) + a **24/7 live loop**;
   these monetize far better per view and feed the algorithm. Near-zero extra cost
   (just stitch existing clips).
3. → **Parent email list / printables / app / membership** — the real margin.
Every Short ends on the soft loop + "Come learn with Willow!"

## Distribution: produce once, post everywhere
One 9:16 master → YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, **Facebook Reels**
(pays well for family content), Pinterest (huge for parents), plus 16:9/1:1 re-cuts.
Multiplies reach for ~0 extra art/voice cost. Later: **second-language channels**
("Aprende con Willow" — same art, re-voiced) ≈ 2–3× audience for ~10% more work.

## 18-month plan (phased, honest)
**Phase 0 — Foundation (now, this repo).** Lock brand, character, family voices,
pipeline, first 20+ scripts; claim handles on every platform; stand up the parent
email capture.

**Phase 1 — Volume & signal (Mo 1–3).** 1 Short/day on every platform (batch weekly).
Find the 2–3 formats that pop (use Higgsfield `virality_predictor` + real retention).
Launch first long-form compilations + a 24/7 live loop. Ship a free printable to grow
the email list. Target: first ~10k subs + a viral hit.

**Phase 2 — Monetize the audience (Mo 4–9).** YPP on; **sponsorships** begin (10k–100k
range closes deals routinely and sponsorships *bypass* COPPA). Paid printable packs +
KDP activity/coloring books. Distribute **Willow songs** to Spotify/Apple Music (kids
music streams forever). Open **The Willow Club** (parent-facing). Target: $5–15k/mo.

**Phase 3 — Scale the IP (Mo 10–18).** Merch (plush Willow, POD apparel), a simple
learning app/game, licensing inquiries, second-language channels, and a tiny content
team so output scales past one family. Target: **$60k/mo run-rate from a portfolio**
of streams — none of them individually fragile.

## What makes this work vs. what kills it
**Works because:** studio-grade art/voice/animation is now this toolchain; the niche
is evergreen and enormous; the character + multi-stream model is exactly how every
kids brand that hit this number did it.
**Dies if:** we lean on Shorts ad money (too low), cheat COPPA (illegal), chase trends
instead of building Willow's world (no asset accrues), or post inconsistently (the
algorithm *and* parents punish gaps). Consistency + a beloved character + stacked
revenue is the whole thesis.
